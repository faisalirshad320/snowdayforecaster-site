How much snow closes school in Grand Rapids?

In the Grand Rapids area it takes roughly 6.2 inches of new snow for the chance of a school closing to reach 50%, and about 11.1 inches for 80%, for a plain overnight snowfall with no ice and no snow already down. Cold alone starts closing schools around -35°F wind chill.

West Michigan gets heavy lake-effect snow off Lake Michigan, so Grand Rapids-area districts — GRPS especially — close or run two-hour delays regularly through January and February, more often than the state's east side.

When Grand Rapids snow days happen

Closures cluster from mid-December through February, with the first of the season usually in late November or early December and a few from late-season blizzards into March.

Make-up days and remote days in Michigan

Michigan requires 180 days and 1,098 hours, but the first six cancelled days are forgiven automatically and districts can apply to waive three more. That cushion is why Michigan districts close without much hesitation early in the winter and get stricter in March.

Rules change and districts apply them differently; treat this as background, not policy.
