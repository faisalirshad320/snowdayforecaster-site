Is there school on MLK Day?

MLK Day 2027 falls on Monday, January 18, 2027. Here is whether schools close, and why it differs between districts.

Short answer: In most districts, no. Martin Luther King Jr. Day is a federal holiday and the large majority of US public school districts close for it. A smaller number stay open, and some use it as a scheduled snow make-up day.

Is MLK Day a federal holiday?

MLK Day is a federal holiday (one of the eleven set by 5 U.S.C. 6103), so federal offices, post offices and most banks are closed. Federal status does not decide whether schools close, though: public school calendars are set by each district (and in some states by the state), so the same holiday can be a day off in one district and a normal school day in the next.

Federal offices, the Postal Service and Federal Reserve banks follow the federal holiday list; private businesses and individual banks set their own hours. Stock markets follow the NYSE holiday calendar.

How school districts handle MLK Day

Most large urban and suburban districts close for MLK Day. Where a district has lost several days to winter weather, its calendar may name MLK Day as an emergency make-up day — check the district's published calendar for a line such as 'make-up day if needed'.

MLK Day and snow days

MLK Day falls in the heart of winter, often during the coldest week of the year. A storm over the long weekend can extend the closure into Tuesday, and extreme wind chill is a common reason for that.

How to check your own district

  • The district calendar is the only authoritative source. Most districts publish it as a PDF on their website before the school year starts.
  • Look for make-up-day notes. Calendars often mark holidays that will be reclaimed if too many snow days are used.
  • Private and charter schools set their own calendars and often differ from the local public district.
